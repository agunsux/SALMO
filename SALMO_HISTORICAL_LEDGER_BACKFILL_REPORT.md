## 1. Executive Summary & Non-Negotiable Core Principle

In quantitative sports modeling, the single greatest source of false confidence is retroactive prediction fabrication (lookahead bias and simulated hindsight). Many commercial platforms backtest models against historical data and present those results to users as if they were live pre-match calls.

**Salmo rejects this practice unequivocally.**

> **The Salmo Historical Backfill Principle:**  
> *Past matches may be recorded in the ledger for score verification, model training, and baseline statistics. However, under no circumstances will Salmo present a historical match as a pre-match prediction unless that prediction was demonstrably generated, timestamped, and stored before the kickoff whistle.*

To enforce this principle architecturally, the Salmo Shadow Ledger categorizes every single record into one of **four explicit, mutually exclusive provenance tiers** (plus a live category for daily signals).

## 3. Data Source Provenance & Forensic Invariants

### 3.1 Genuine Historical Predictions (`HISTORICAL_PREDICTION`: 30 records)
- **Source Artifact:** `data/verification/live_prediction_ledger.jsonl`
- **Time Horizon:** 2026-10-15 to 2026-11-08
- **Content:** 30 predictions evaluated and persisted before kickoff across Premier League, La Liga, and Champions League fixtures.
- **Verification Check:** Every record has an authentic `capturedAt` timestamp preceding `kickoffAt` by at least 90 minutes. Closing line snapshots and CLV computations are stored where available.
- **Settlement Status:** 29 settled, 1 pending.

### 3.2 Controlled Historical Reconstructions (`HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION`: 60 records)
- **Source Artifact:** `data/external/football-data/2526_E1.csv` (Premier League 2025/2026 early season).
- **Invariants Enforced:**
  1. `lookaheadBiasVerified = true`
  2. `featureCutoffTimestamp = matchDate + "T10:00:00Z"` (strictly prior to kickoff at 15:00/17:30 UTC).
  3. Features utilized are limited strictly to preceding round statistics. Match outcomes, cards, and live odds movements are completely absent from the feature set.
  4. Odds utilized are opening/early Bet365 lines recorded in the dataset.

### 3.3 Verified Match Results Only (`HISTORICAL_RESULT_ONLY`: 522 records)
- **Source Artifact:** Historical football-data archive (2024–2026 seasons).
- **Strict Invariants:**
  ```typescript
  stakeUnits: 0.0,
  profitUnits: 0.0,
  predictionStatus: 'NO_MARKET_DATA',
  reasoning: 'Historical match result record only. No pre-match prediction evaluated.'
  ```
- **Audit Verification:** An automated script scanned all 522 records; 100% have `stakeUnits === 0` and `profitUnits === 0`. No backtest result can corrupt Salmo's transparent track record.

### 3.4 Quarantined Anomalies (`UNVERIFIED`: 1 record)
- **Record Identifier:** `unverified_EPL_2025_SAMPLE_CONFLICT`
- **Anomaly Type:** Intentionally injected synthetic conflict test record verifying that ambiguous fixtures are placed in quarantine rather than contaminating clean datasets.
- **Quarantine Invariant:** Never queried by production services; flagged with badge `QUARANTINED` in Operator Dashboard.
